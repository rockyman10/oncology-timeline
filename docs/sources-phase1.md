## Notes for review

- Link-only stub PMIDs: CLOT, CATCH, AVERT, CASSINI, and CANVAS PMIDs match the anticoagulation timeline records. Hokusai VTE Cancer (29231094), SELECT-D (29746227), ADAM-VTE (31630479), Caravaggio (32223112), and API-CAT (40162636) have no PMID on the anticoagulation timeline; these were resolved by DOI search in PubMed on 2026-11-24.
- ALSYMPCA abstract: one sentence reads "radium-233"; the card says radium-223 throughout, as the rest of the abstract does.
- CALGB/SWOG 80405 abstract: the response-rate difference is printed as "4.4%, 95% CI, 1.0%-9.0%, P = .13". The interval and the P value do not agree as printed, so the card gives the two response rates and the P value only.
- PREVAIL abstract does not state castration status; the card describes the population as the abstract and title do (metastatic prostate cancer before chemotherapy).
- STAMPEDE (abiraterone) is one comparison within a multi-arm platform; the card covers only the 2017 abiraterone comparison.
- ATAC confidence intervals after the first are printed in brackets without a "95% CI" label in the abstract; the card keeps the bracket form for those.
- PACIFIC: overall survival was a coprimary end point but was unplanned for this interim analysis and is not in the abstract. The duration-of-response sentence reports the percentage with an ongoing response at 18 months, not a median duration in months.
- KEYNOTE-522: pathological complete response is from the first 602 patients; event-free survival uses the full assigned groups (784 vs 390) and has no P value in the abstract.
- KEYNOTE-177: overall survival remained blinded at this analysis. The 13.7 versus 10.8 month figures are restricted mean survival after 24 months, not median overall survival. Crossover from chemotherapy to pembrolizumab after progression was allowed.
- CASPIAN: this paper reports only the durvalumab plus platinum-etoposide versus platinum-etoposide comparison; the durvalumab-tremelimumab arm is not in the abstract. The control group could receive more chemotherapy cycles and prophylactic cranial irradiation. The abstract prints "p=0.0047]);" with an extra bracket; the card uses p=0.0047.
- ADRIATIC: the durvalumab-tremelimumab group remains blinded. The overall survival confidence interval is 98.321% and the progression-free survival interval is 97.195%.
- A later immunotherapy phase can still add other immunotherapy trials. These six (KEYNOTE-522, PACIFIC, IMpower133, CASPIAN, ADRIATIC, and KEYNOTE-177) are included in their tumor lanes in this phase 1 pass.
